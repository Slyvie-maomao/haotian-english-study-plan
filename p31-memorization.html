Grammar Map

比较级与最高级：先认结构，再记变形

下面的规则直接对应课文中的 taller, stronger, most famous, most popular, most successful 和 shortest。

比较级：两者比较
A + be + comparative + than + B

Batman is taller and stronger than ordinary men.
两个比较级比较同一个对象时，可以共用后面的 than + B。

最高级：三者或更多之中最……
the + superlative + noun

the shortest actor；如果前面已有 his 等物主限定词，就不再加 the：His most popular films。

填词判断：先圈标志词，再决定级别

原级：as + 形容词原级 + as 表示“一样……”；not as + 形容词原级 + as 表示“不如……”。

比较级：than；much / far / a lot / a little / even + 比较级；of the two。

最高级：in the class / world；of all / the three；one of + the + 最高级 + 复数名词；ever / I know / of all time。

先判断是在比较“两者”，还是在一个范围内找“最……”，再按词尾规则变形。
课内改写：A 没有 B 大

相同：A is as big as B.

不及：A isn't as big as B. 中间必须用原级 big。

A is smaller than B. = B is bigger than A.

比较电影院座位数时还可以说：A has fewer seats than B. / B has more seats than A.
一般短词
tall → taller → tallest
直接加 -er / -est。课文还有 stronger, shortest。
以 e 结尾
large → larger → largest
只加 -r / -st。
辅音字母 + y
happy → happier → happiest
把 y 变成 i，再加 -er / -est。
重读闭音节
big → bigger → biggest
末尾“辅音—元音—辅音”时，双写最后一个辅音字母。
较长形容词
famous → more famous → most famous
词前加 more / most。课文还有 popular, successful。
不规则变化
good → better → best
bad → worse → worst；far → farther/further → farthest/furthest。
课文原句 1 · 比较级并列

Batman is a superhero who is taller and stronger than ordinary men.

taller 和 stronger 共用 than ordinary men。
课文原句 2 · one of 最高级

Tim Burton is one of the most famous American film directors.

one of + the + 最高级 + 复数名词，所以是 directors。
课文原句 3 · 物主限定词 + 最高级

His most popular films include ...

His 已经限定名词，不能再说 His the most popular films。
课文原句 4 · the + 最高级

He was in fact the shortest actor ever to play Batman.

short → shortest；最高级前保留 the。

背诵时最容易错：不能说 more taller 或 the most shortest；比较级和最高级标志不能重复。

本单元的比较级加强词：much / far / a lot + 比较级 表示“……得多”，a little + 比较级 表示“稍微……一点”。

01

身份与成就：先下结论，再给证据

这一段回答：Tim Burton 是谁？哪些作品和奖项能证明？

Tim Burton is one of the most famous American film directors.

His most popular films include: Batman (1989), Edward Scissorhands (1990), Batman Returns (1992) and Dumbo (2019).

In 1990, he got a Daytime Emmy Award for his cartoon series Beetlejuice and in 1991 he got a Hugo Award for Edward Scissorhands.

◫教材原文已遮盖
先用下面的逻辑线索完整背诵

教材原文与示范朗读

先听一遍，再遮盖原文跟读。背完以后才查看全文核对。

1逻辑顺序

第一句下结论，第二句用电影证明受欢迎，第三句再用奖项证明实力。

  1. 结论著名导演之一one of the most famous ... film directors
  2. 作品证据四部代表作 + 年份His most popular films include: ...
  3. 奖项证据 11990 EmmyIn 1990 ... Daytime Emmy Award ... Beetlejuice
  4. 奖项证据 21991 Hugoand in 1991 ... Hugo Award ... Edward Scissorhands
2句首启动

看句首说出完整句，不要只念省略号前面的部分。

Tim Burton is one of ...

His most popular films include: ...

In 1990, he got ... and in 1991 he got ...

02

成长与选择：兴趣怎样变成自己的电影

这一段回答：童年的输入和尝试，怎样一步步变成学习、工作与独立创作？

Tim grew up watching horror films and cartoons.

He read Edgar Allan Poe's horror stories and he loved drawing.

He also made short films in his back garden.

After leaving school, Burton studied animation.

Then, he got a job at Walt Disney studios.

He was a very good animator, but he didn't draw in the Disney style.

So he left Disney and started to make his own films.

◫教材原文已遮盖
先沿着“输入—尝试—学习—冲突—选择”复述

教材原文与示范朗读

听清过去式，并重读 but 和 So，它们决定故事转折。

1逻辑顺序

前五句按时间推进，最后两句用“但是 → 所以”解释他为什么独立。

  1. 童年输入看 + 读grew up watching ... / read ... horror stories
  2. 主动尝试画 + 拍短片loved drawing / made short films ...
  3. 系统学习学习动画After leaving school ... studied animation
  4. 进入行业进入 DisneyThen ... got a job at Walt Disney studios
  5. 冲突不符合 Disney 风格but ... didn't draw in the Disney style
  6. 结果离开，拍自己的电影So ... left Disney / started to make his own films
2句首启动

句首本身就是时间与因果路标。

Tim grew up ...

He read ... and he loved ...

He also made ...

After leaving school, ...

Then, he got ...

He was ..., but ...

So he left ... and started ...

03

争议与反转：不被看好的选择成功了

这一段回答：大家为什么不看好 Michael Keaton？事实和结果是什么？

One of his most successful films is Batman.

At first, people were upset when Tim Burton chose the comedy actor Michael Keaton to play Batman.

Batman is a superhero who is taller and stronger than ordinary men, and Michael Keaton was too short.

At 1.75 m, he was in fact the shortest actor ever to play Batman.

However, the film was very successful and everybody loved Michael Keaton as Batman.

◫教材原文已遮盖
先说清“成功影片—选角争议—原因—反转”

教材原文与示范朗读

读出 At first 与 However 的前后反转。

1逻辑顺序

先告诉读者电影成功，再回到最初的质疑，最后用结果反转质疑。

  1. 结果预告最成功的电影之一One of ... most successful films ... Batman
  2. 最初反应选喜剧演员，大家不满At first ... were upset / chose ... to play Batman
  3. 为什么Batman 应更高、更强taller and stronger than ... / too short
  4. 事实1.75 m，历来最矮At 1.75 m, ... in fact ... the shortest actor ever to play Batman
  5. 结果反转影片成功，观众喜欢However / very successful / everybody loved ... as Batman
2句首启动

用两个转折路标 At first 和 However 固定整段骨架。

One of his most successful films is ...

At first, people were upset when ...

Batman is a superhero who is ..., and ...

At 1.75 m, he was in fact ...

However, the film was ... and everybody ...

三段连背：沿着一个故事走完全程

不是把三段当成 15 个互不相关的句子。全文在回答三个连续问题：他是谁？他怎样成为这样的人？哪件事证明他的独特选择最终成功？

🎬WHO
第一段：先建立人物

著名导演 → 用四部电影证明受欢迎 → 用两个奖项证明实力。

↓ 接着追问：这样的导演是怎样成长起来的？
🌱HOW
第二段：再解释成长路线

童年输入 → 自己动手 → 学习动画 → 进入 Disney → 风格冲突 → 走自己的路。

↓ 接着追问：哪部电影最能证明他的选择虽然特别，却可能成功？
🦇TEST
第三段：用 Batman 完成验证

成功影片 → 选角引发不满 → 身高看似不合适 → However，电影和演员都成功。

第 1 轮中文逻辑 + 英文句首

WHO：Tim Burton is one of ... → His most popular films ... → In 1990 ... and in 1991 ...

HOW：Tim grew up ... → He read ... → He also made ... → After leaving school ... → Then ... → but ... → So ...

TEST：One of ... → At first ... → Batman is ... → At 1.75 m ... → However ...
